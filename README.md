# For the Grieving

A tender page of Scripture for those who are grieving — comfort for everyone missing someone tonight.

- "Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted." (Matthew 5:4)
- The Lord is near to the brokenhearted (Psalm 34:18)
- He heals the brokenhearted (Psalm 147:3)
- We do not grieve as those who have no hope (1 Thessalonians 4:13-14)
- No more tears (Revelation 21:4)
- Jesus wept too (John 11:35)

Built with Muse artifacts. All Scripture from the World English Bible (public domain).
